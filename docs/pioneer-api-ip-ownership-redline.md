# Pioneer API — IP ownership redline position

**Status:** Negotiation aid; counsel should convert this into tracked changes in Pioneer’s contract.

## Proposed replacement language

> Pioneer owns the machine-specific and Discus-specific work product expressly identified in the Statement of Work and created for Pioneer under it, including the agreed integration code, API schemas, configuration, mappings, runbook, and final evaluation artifacts, upon payment of the applicable fees.
>
> Contractor retains all right, title, and interest in pre-existing materials and reusable background technology, including generic parsers, geometry and registration algorithms, generic AI-provider adapters, test harnesses, templates, tools, know-how, and independently developed improvements that do not contain or disclose Pioneer Confidential Information. To the extent retained background technology is embedded in or required to use the paid deliverables, Contractor grants Pioneer a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use, execute, modify, and maintain that background technology solely as part of or in support of Pioneer’s internal use of the deliverables.
>
> Pioneer Data and Pioneer Confidential Information remain Pioneer’s property. Contractor receives no right to use them except to perform the Statement of Work and may not use them to train a general-purpose model, publish them, or disclose them without written authorization.

## Negotiation notes

- Avoid an assignment of “all inventions, tools, methods, and improvements” without a background-technology carve-out.
- Preserve Pioneer’s ownership of its machine/Discus deliverable and its data; do not claim ownership of Pioneer’s pre-existing systems.
- Keep the license broad enough that Pioneer can operate and maintain the delivered integration.
- Make the ownership transfer conditional on payment, while confidentiality and data-protection duties begin on access.
- Add a written-publicity restriction and a return/deletion obligation for source files, images, credentials, and generated artifacts.
