# Pioneer API booking packet

**Status:** Pre-signature working drafts; review with Pioneer and counsel before execution.

## Commercial proposal at a glance

- **Engagement:** A controlled pilot to evaluate and implement the integration boundary between Pioneer’s machine/software workflow and the agreed Pioneer systems.
- **Pilot fee:** $8,500 fixed, billed as five milestone payments ($1,155 / $1,935 / $2,320 / $1,935 / $1,155), each released when its deliverable is accepted.
- **Nominal basis:** about 74 hours at $115/hour; the milestone payments, not hours worked, are the contract basis.
- **Authorized contingency:** up to $1,500 (about 13 hours) at $115/hour, only for approved in-scope uncertainty or change work with written approval.
- **Not-to-exceed ceiling:** $10,000 without a signed change order.
- **Condition:** The quote depends on receiving one approved real `source.iwp` plus corresponding bubbled PDF/PNG pair. It does not guarantee production success before that validation.
- **Provider boundary:** Synthetic proof uses Google Gemini through AI SDK. Real Pioneer data uses only Pioneer-authorized Claude through GovCloud, subject to Pioneer’s security approval.

## Bring to the booking conversation

1. Statement of Work draft.
2. Billable quote and milestone/acceptance schedule.
3. IP ownership and reusable-background-technology redline.
4. NDA, data-handling, AI-provider, retention, and deletion terms.
5. One-page technical brief and architecture diagram.
6. Information-request checklist for the machine, Discus, IWP files, outputs, network, and security constraints.
7. Change-order, payment, independent-contractor, and termination terms.
8. W-9 and vendor-onboarding information.
9. A clear list of assumptions, exclusions, and open decisions.

## Decisions to obtain from Pioneer

- Legal contracting entity, billing contact, procurement process, and payment terms.
- Authorized technical owner and authorized approver.
- The exact machine model/software version and Discus product/version.
- One approved, redacted or otherwise authorized real file pair.
- Whether the pilot may use Pioneer’s GovCloud Claude access and which endpoint/model is approved.
- Network boundary: local-only, VPN, private cloud, or authorized outbound service.
- Required retention, audit, export-control, and customer-data handling rules.
- Acceptance criteria for the pilot and who signs acceptance.
- Confirmation of the fixed milestone payment schedule and who accepts each milestone.

## Do not promise at booking

Do not promise automatic production success, a guaranteed throughput increase, or a success-fee outcome before real-data validation. Do not put private statements about Pioneer’s budget, prior spending, or internal bottleneck in the client-facing contract. Frame the opportunity as a high-value operational problem while pricing the defined work and risk, not an unverified assumption about ability to pay.
