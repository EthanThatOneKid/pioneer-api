# Pioneer API — acceptance and commercial terms draft

**Status:** Draft for negotiation; not legal advice.

## Effort and ceiling

- Rate: **$175/hour**.
- Planned effort: **88 hours**.
- Authorized contingency: **12 hours**, used only for approved in-scope uncertainty or written change work.
- Not-to-exceed ceiling: **$17,500** without a signed change order.
- The estimate is conditional on one approved real source IWP and bubbled PDF/PNG pair. It is not a production-success guarantee.

## Invoicing recommendation

Use milestone or biweekly invoices with hours and deliverables itemized. A practical starting position is invoicing at completion of each accepted milestone, with the final invoice including the audit manifest and handoff. Net-15 or Net-30, reimbursable expenses, taxes, and payment method remain procurement decisions and should be written into the final agreement.

## Acceptance

Pioneer names one technical approver. The approver has five business days after each delivery to accept it or identify specific material defects against the agreed acceptance criteria. Silence should not be deemed acceptance unless Pioneer’s contract process allows it. Corrections to an accepted-scope defect are included in the estimate; new features, new systems, new geometries, new environments, or production deployment require a change order.

## Change orders

A change order states the requested change, reason, additional hours, price ceiling, schedule impact, acceptance criteria, IP/data impact, and required approvals. Work stops at the ceiling if approval is not received. Contractor may recommend parking lower-priority work to stay within the ceiling.

## Independent-contractor terms

The final agreement should cover independent-contractor status, taxes, insurance, equipment, location, non-exclusivity, confidentiality, conflicts, work product, termination notice, payment for authorized work performed, and expense approval.
