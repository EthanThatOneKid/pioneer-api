# Pioneer API — vendor onboarding checklist

## Pioneer should provide

- Vendor registration/procurement portal and required forms.
- Legal contracting entity and purchase-order requirements.
- W-9 request instructions and tax/vendor classification.
- ACH or payment setup requirements; do not send banking details by email unless Pioneer requires its approved portal.
- Certificate-of-insurance requirements, if any.
- NDA, master services agreement, SOW, data-processing/security addendum, and IP templates.
- Supplier-security questionnaire and export-control/ITAR acknowledgments.
- Invoice submission address, required PO number, payment terms, and approver.

## Contractor should bring

- Completed W-9 with legal name and tax classification.
- Business/contact information and invoice template.
- Proof of insurance only if required and actually held.
- Signed NDA and SOW only after reviewing final terms.
- Security practices summary: no secrets in Git, least privilege, approved storage, deletion at closeout.
- Technical brief, architecture, information-request checklist, quote, and milestone acceptance schedule.

## Before signing

Confirm the final agreement names the contracting entity, rate, ceiling, invoice terms, ownership split, background-technology license, confidentiality/data rules, approved AI provider, acceptance process, change orders, termination, and governing-law/venue terms. Have counsel review any broad work-made-for-hire, perpetual exclusivity, unlimited indemnity, uncapped liability, or warranty language.
